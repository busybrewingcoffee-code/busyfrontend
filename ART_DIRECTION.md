# Menu illustration references

The supplied menu is the recipe authority. References below inform recognizable visual cues, not changes to ingredients, portions, or serving vessels. Cups and glasses are illustrative; actual stall serveware is unconfirmed.

## Visual decisions

- Classics: dark Americano, broad foamy cappuccino, pale milky latte with latte art, chocolate mocha, and tall blended cold coffee. Silhouette, surface, and fill all contribute.
- Signature: strawberry base; dark Vietnamese coffee over condensed milk; popcorn cloud; thick cocoa-dusted tiramisu cream; orange juice with a dark espresso cap; cranberry fizz with espresso; dark cola with vanilla foam and bubbles.
- Matcha: hot ceramic matcha, iced green/milk layers, strawberry pink/milk/green layers, and caramel ribbons. Green consistently identifies matcha.
- Pujo: date-palm jaggery cues for both nolen gur coffees, distinct cloud topping, and raw mango/lime-green cooler. No roasted-mango claim is made for this menu's syrup-based shorbot.
- Mocktails: purple jamun, mint/lime mojito, orange/cranberry sunset, dark spiced cola, pale strawberry lemonade. Ingredients, bubbles, and silhouettes differentiate these from espresso drinks.
- Food: plain fries, red seasoning, cheese ribbons; momo filling cutaways for vegetables, paneer, chicken; contrasting roll fillings; round falafel versus oblong kebab. Fried momo receives a browned wrapper.

All illustrations share warm muted colors, burgundy-brown outlines, restrained shadows, rounded strokes, and the same SVG coordinate system. Twenty-four drink configurations and ten food variants cover all 34 menu entries. Saved basket entries resolve current art by stable menu ID.
