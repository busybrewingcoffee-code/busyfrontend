# Design context: Busy Brewing staff counter

- Audience: 2-3 friends running a pop-up coffee stall (Horn OK Please, Delhi). Mainly one iPad at the counter, sometimes an iPhone. Used during rushes; speed and calm matter more than decoration.
- Jobs: tap drinks into an order (hot/iced, add-ons as lines), record UPI/cash, move tickets New → Brewing → Done, check day-wise sales.
- Personality: calm, warm, editorial, quietly premium. Reference: the earlier Pujo staff app (src/style.css): warm greige tiles, cream paper, burgundy (#750f2e) used sparingly, Playfair Display headings, DM Sans body, small letter-spaced caps labels, flat outlined line illustrations, hairline dividers, generous whitespace.
- Event flavour ("The Season Between" winter menu) is a light accent only; never loud stripes, glows or saturated reds.
- Real logo only (public/brand.jpg / brand-logo.png); never a typed stand-in.
